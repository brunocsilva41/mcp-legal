Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how MCP Video Publisher ("Application", "we", "us") handles user data, access tokens, and media content when integrating with third-party developer platforms, specifically the TikTok for Developers API and Google YouTube Data API.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We respect your privacy by design. MCP Video Publisher operates primarily as a local developer tool and MCP server:

  • We do not collect, store, sell, or rent personal data to third parties.
  • We do not maintain centralized databases of user videos, personal identifiers, or profile activities.
  • We do not utilize external tracking cookies or invasive telemetry scripts.

3. Authentication & API Scopes Used

To deliver automated video publishing, the Application requests explicit consent via official OAuth 2.0 mechanisms:

  • TikTok Scopes:
    • user.info.basic: Read basic account information and profile verification strictly to display the publishing channel and query posting parameters (such as privacy options and duration restrictions via creator_info/query).
    • video.publish: Authorized strictly to initiate direct uploads of creator-specified MP4 files to the user's verified TikTok account upon user request.
  • Token Storage: OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the user's machine (within the protected secrets/ folder) and are only transmitted directly to the official platform OAuth endpoints.

4. Third-Party Services

When you interact with integrated platforms, your data handling is also subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

Because no user data is retained on external remote servers by MCP Video Publisher:

  • Local Token Deletion: You can delete all authentication tokens instantly at any time by removing your local token files located in the project's secrets/ directory.
  • Permission Revocation: You can revoke the Application's access at any time through your TikTok Account Settings > Security > Manage App Permissions or Google Account Permissions.

6. Security

We enforce secure protocols (HTTPS / TLS) for all communication with official platform APIs and encourage users to maintain secure local operating environments.
